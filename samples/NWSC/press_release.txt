Northwind Systems Reports First Quarter Fiscal 2026 Results

REDMOND, Wash. - October 28, 2025 - Northwind Systems Corp. today announced the following results for the quarter ended September 30, 2025, as compared to the corresponding period of last fiscal year:

· Revenue was $18.6 billion and increased 14%
· Operating income was $7.9 billion and increased 17%
· Net income was $6.4 billion and increased 12%
· Diluted earnings per share was $2.31 and increased 13%

Segment results:
· Revenue in Cloud Platforms was $8.2 billion and increased 24%
· Revenue in Business Applications was $6.1 billion and increased 9%
· Revenue in Devices was $4,300 million and increased 3%

Northwind returned $4.1 billion to shareholders in the form of share repurchases and dividends in the first quarter.
